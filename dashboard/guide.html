🔍 What Gets Analyzed?

VendorGuard AI automatically checks for these common fraud and compliance issues:

🏦

Bank Account Mismatch

Does the invoice bank account match the verified supplier account?

📑

Duplicate Invoices

Are there two invoices with the same number? (could mean double payment)

⚡

Payment Conflicts

Does the invoice say "unpaid" but a receipt says "settled"?

🔗

Missing PO Reference

Does the invoice reference the approved purchase order?

⏰

Overdue Invoices

Is the invoice past its due date? (pressure tactic?)

👤

Supplier Identity

Do all documents refer to the same verified supplier?

💰

Amount Consistency

Do amounts match across PO, invoice, delivery, and receipt?

🔒

PDPA Compliance

All bank accounts are automatically masked for data protection.

🎯 Understanding the Risk Score

After analysis, you'll get a score from 0 to 100:

0–29
Low Risk
Documents look good. Safe to process payment.
30–49
Medium Risk
Minor issues found. Review before processing.
50–100
High Risk
Significant issues. Payment should be blocked until verified.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install any software?

No! Everything runs in your web browser through Google Colab. Just click the link and run.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Bank accounts are automatically masked (only last 4 digits shown). No personal data is stored after the session ends.

How many files can I upload at once?

There's no hard limit. We recommend uploading all documents from one transaction together (e.g., PO + invoice + receipt) for the best analysis.

What if I only have one document?

That works too! The system will classify it and extract key fields. However, cross-document checks (like matching bank accounts) work best with multiple related documents.

Can I use this for any supplier or country?

Currently optimized for Malaysian AP documents (MYR currency, SST tax, Company Registration numbers). The core logic works with any supplier.
